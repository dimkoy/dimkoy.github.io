## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the Nuclear Wars application (the "Service"). It explains what little information the Service touches, how it is used, and the privacy rights available to you. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

The short version: **Nuclear Wars does not collect personal data and has no accounts or advertising.** To improve the game, it sends anonymous usage statistics (for example, "training completed" or "purchase screen opened") tied to a random identifier of the installation, not to you.

## Definitions

Capitalized terms have the following meanings, whether they appear in singular or plural:

- **Application / Service:** Nuclear Wars, a strategy game for Apple Vision Pro developed by Dmitrii Cherviakov.
- **Company:** Dmitrii Cherviakov (referred to as "we," "us," or "our").
- **Country:** Spain.
- **You:** The individual using the Service.
- **Game Center:** Apple's social gaming service, operated by Apple Inc., used by the Service for optional multiplayer matches and leaderboards.
- **Personal Data:** Any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.

## Data We Collect

The Service:

- does not require or offer account registration;
- does not collect names, email addresses, or contact information;
- does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), advertising or tracking of any kind;
- collects anonymous usage statistics, described below.

**Usage statistics.** The Service records gameplay and menu events such as starting or completing the training, starting or finishing a match (number of opponents and the result), opening or closing the purchase screen, and the outcome of a purchase or restore attempt. Each event carries a random identifier created on first launch, the app version, the platform, and whether the full version is unlocked. These statistics are not linked to your identity and are not used for tracking or advertising.

- On iPhone and iPad, statistics are processed by **Google Analytics for Firebase** (Google LLC), without the advertising identifier. Google may derive an approximate location (country/city) from the IP address. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
- On Apple Vision Pro, statistics are sent to a server operated by the Company. The server keeps the country of the request and does not store the IP address.

Statistics processed by Google Analytics for Firebase are retained by Google for up to 14 months. Statistics sent to the Company's server are kept for as long as they are needed to understand how players use the game and to improve it. They are used for no other purpose.

## In-App Purchases

The full version is sold as an in-app purchase through the App Store. Payments are processed by Apple; the Company never receives your payment details. The Service records whether the full version is unlocked (and, in the statistics above, whether a purchase succeeded) but no payment information.

## Multiplayer, Leaderboards and Game Center

Optional multiplayer matches use **Apple Game Center** and Apple's matchmaking infrastructure. When you choose to play multiplayer:

- Your Game Center player identifier and display name are made visible to the other players in your match (for example, in the match lobby). This information is provided and processed by Apple, not by the Company.
- Match data (unit orders, game state) is exchanged directly between the participating devices through Apple's GameKit framework. The Company does not operate any multiplayer servers and has no access to this data.
- Your use of Game Center is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

If you are signed in to Game Center, the result of each completed match (the number of your surviving citizens and the number of opponents) is submitted to Game Center leaderboards. Leaderboard scores, together with your Game Center player name, are stored and displayed by Apple to other Game Center players, including in challenges. The Company does not receive or store this data.

If you do not sign in to Game Center, multiplayer and leaderboards are simply unavailable; the rest of the game is unaffected.

## Data Stored on Your Device

Game settings (such as interface preferences) are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Children's Privacy

The Service does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. The Service is rated for age 12+ due to its war-themed content.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

The Company does not sell, share, or disclose data to affiliates, partners, advertisers, or authorities. The only third parties involved are Apple (Game Center and in-app purchases) and, on iPhone and iPad, Google (anonymous usage statistics), as described above.

## Security

No data leaves your device except the Game Center exchange and leaderboard scores, which are protected by Apple's platform security, and the anonymous usage statistics described above. Nevertheless, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, and the Company cannot guarantee the absolute security of data handled by third-party platforms.

## International Transfers

Anonymous usage statistics may be processed on servers located outside your country: on iPhone and iPad by Google under its own policies and safeguards. Any processing connected to Game Center and in-app purchases is performed by Apple under its own policies and safeguards.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location (including under the GDPR if you are in the European Economic Area), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because the Company holds no data that identifies you — usage statistics are tied only to a random installation identifier — there is typically nothing for us to act on; requests concerning Game Center data should be directed to Apple. You can nevertheless contact us with any privacy question using the details below.
